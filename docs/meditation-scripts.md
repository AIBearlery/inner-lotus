# Inner Lotus — Morning Meditation Scripts (v1)

Six short, spoken-register scripts for Amelie's morning sessions. They're written to be
**read aloud and recorded** (by you, or a loved one) — natural, warm, unhurried. The user
sees the short *seed* on screen first (eyes open), then closes their eyes and only hears the
voice.

## How to record (quick + good enough)
- A quiet room and a phone is plenty. Sit close-ish to the mic; avoid echoey rooms.
- Read **slowly**. Where it says `[pause — N breaths]`, actually stop and breathe; those
  silences are the meditation. Don't rush them.
- One mistake? Just pause and re-read the line — easy to trim later.
- Save each as its own file and name it exactly as the **filename** below, then drop them in
  `assets/audio/`. That's all — the app is already wired to pick them up.
- Target lengths are a guide, not a rule. A little longer is fine.

> Tone: gentle, never solemn or "performy." Think of speaking softly to someone you love who
> just woke up. Morning sessions should *lift and steady*, never deflate.

## 1. Wake the Body · `wake-the-body.m4a` · ~4 min
*Gentle activation — for a Bright/Light, energising start.*

**Seed (on screen):** "A tree doesn’t brace for spring. It just lets the sap rise, one warm inch at a time. Let’s wake like that — no force, only rising."

**Script:**
Let’s begin. Settle where you are, and let your eyes close.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Take one slow breath in through the nose… and let it go, softly.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Bring a little attention to your feet. Just notice them. Give your toes a gentle wiggle, waking them up.
`[pause]`
Now your hands. Open them slowly, and close them. Feel the blood moving there.
`[pause]`
Roll your shoulders back, once, easily. Let them drop.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Take a fuller breath now — in… lifting the chest a little… and out.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Good. Nothing to force this morning. You’re just letting the light in, one inch at a time.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
One more easy breath. And when you’re ready, let your eyes open. Gently — you’re awake, and you’re here.

## 2. Spark · `spark.m4a` · ~3 min
*Energising breath — for a Bright, awake lift. Keep it light; never strain.*

**Seed (on screen):** "The morning breeze has secrets to tell you. Don’t go back to sleep." — Rumi

**Script:**
Sit up tall, and close your eyes. We’re going to wake the body up a little, gently.
`[pause]`
Breathe in through the nose, filling up… and out through the mouth, with a soft sigh. Let it be audible.
`[pause — 1 breath]`
Again — in through the nose… and a long sigh out.
`[pause — 1 breath]`
Two more, at your own pace. Full breath in… and let it go.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Now let the breath return to normal, and just feel the buzz — that quiet aliveness in your chest and hands.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
There it is. That’s your spark. It was there all along; we just fanned it.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Carry it with you. Open your eyes when you’re ready.

## 3. Clear the Lens · `clear-the-lens.m4a` · ~4 min
*Focus primer — for Focused/Grounded/Steady mornings.*

**Seed (on screen):** "The quieter you become, the more you’re able to hear." — Ram Dass

**Script:**
Let your eyes close, and let the body be still.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
We’re not trying to empty the mind. We’re just going to give it one clear thing to rest on: the breath.
`[pause]`
Find the place where you feel the breath most easily — the nose, or the rise of the chest. Rest your attention there.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
When a thought pulls you away — and it will — that’s fine. Just notice, and come back to the breath. No frustration. Coming back *is* the practice.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
In… and out. One breath at a time. The lens is clearing.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Stay with a few more on your own.
`[pause — 5 breaths]`
Good. Notice how a little quieter you already are. Bring that clarity with you. Open your eyes.

## 4. Open with Warmth · `open-with-warmth.m4a` · ~5 min
*Loving-kindness — for Warm/Open mornings and self-compassion.*

**Seed (on screen):** "Somewhere, someone is hoping today is gentle with you. Let’s begin by being that someone — first for yourself, then for one other."

**Script:**
Close your eyes, and let your breath settle into its own easy rhythm.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Bring a hand to your chest, if you’d like. Feel the warmth of it.
`[pause]`
And silently, to yourself, offer a few simple wishes. *May I be well. May I be easy. May I be kind to myself today.*
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Say them again, slowly, like you mean them. *May I be well… may I be easy…*
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Now bring someone to mind — someone you care about. Picture them there.
`[pause]`
And offer the same to them. *May you be well. May you be easy. May today be gentle with you.*
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Feel how the warmth doesn’t run out by sharing it. It grows.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Let the image go, and rest for a moment in that open, warm feeling.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
When you’re ready, open your eyes — and carry a little of that with you.

## 5. Set the Day · `set-the-day.m4a` · ~4 min
*Intention + look-ahead — for Confidence/Balance/Focus.*

**Seed (on screen):** "The day becomes the shape of the intention you give it. So let’s give it a good one."

**Script:**
Settle in, and let your eyes close.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Before the day fills up, let’s choose its shape. Take a slow breath, and let the noise settle.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Ask yourself, quietly: *how do I want to feel at the end of today?* Don’t force an answer. Just let one arrive.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Now picture one small moment ahead where that could be true. See yourself there, feeling it.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
And gently, form the intention. *Today, I’ll… * — whatever fits. Keep it small and kind.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
And one more thing: name one small thing you’re looking forward to today. However tiny. Let yourself feel a flicker of it.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Good. You’ve given the day a direction. Take a breath, and open your eyes when you’re ready to begin it.

## 6. Steady the Storm · `steady-the-storm.m4a` · ~5 min
*Settle a rough / sleepless / anxious morning — the one calming session. Frame it as "settle so you can start," not "wind down to sleep." Voice extra slow and low here.*

**Seed (on screen):** "Even the roughest night is only weather. And weather passes. Let’s let the wind drop, one long slow breath at a time — enough to begin."

**Script:**
It’s okay. Whatever kind of night that was, you’re here now, and we’ll go slowly.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Let your eyes close. You don’t have to fix anything. We’re just going to help your system settle.
`[pause]`
Breathe in gently through the nose… and now a long, slow breath out — longer than the in-breath. Let it drain all the way.
`[pause — 1 breath]`
Again. In… and a long, slow out. The out-breath is where the calm lives.
`[pause — 1 breath]`
Keep that going at your own pace — in softly, out slowly. I’ll be quiet with you.
`[pause — 5 breaths]`
If the mind races, that’s alright. You’re not doing it wrong. Just keep lengthening the out-breath.
`[pause — 5 breaths]`
Feel your shoulders soften. Your jaw. The storm is just weather. It’s already passing.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
You don’t need to feel wonderful. You only need to feel steady enough to begin. And you’re getting there.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Take one more long breath out. And when you’re ready — no rush at all — open your eyes.

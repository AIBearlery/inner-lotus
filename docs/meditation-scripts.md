# Inner Lotus — Amelie Morning Meditation Scripts (Working v1)

**Status:** working draft for tone development (locked-for-now).
**Direction:** softer, less instructional, no posture command at the start, positive steering
only, more breathing room.

## How to record (quick + good enough)
- A quiet room and a phone is plenty. Read **slowly**; the `[pause — N breaths]` silences are
  the meditation — actually stop and breathe them.
- Save each file with the **exact** filename shown (e.g. `wake-the-body.m4a`), drop it into
  `assets/audio/`, and uncomment its line in `src/content/audioSources.ts`. `.m4a`/`.mp3`/`.wav`
  all work. Until then, that session plays a gentle guided-breathing placeholder.

## Global voice rules
- Begin from where the user already is.
- Do not ask the user to change posture at the start.
- Guide toward the intended state; avoid naming unwanted states.
- Keep Amelie companion-like, not teacher-like.
- Leave meaningful silence.
- Morning sessions should wake and steady, not wind down into sleep.
- Avoid clinical, therapeutic, overly spiritual, or productivity-coded language.

# 1. Wake the Body · `wake-the-body.m4a`
**Purpose:** gentle activation — body and mind easing into the day · **Target:** ~4 min

**Seed:** Morning can begin quietly. A little breath, a little feeling, a little light returning to the body.

**Script:**
Let’s begin where you are.
Let the morning find you for a moment.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Feel the body you woke up in today. Let it be simple.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Take one easy breath in… and let it leave slowly.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Again, gently. Breath in… breath out.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Now bring your attention down to your feet. Simply feel them as they are — the weight of them, the temperature, a tiny bit of aliveness.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Let that awareness travel slowly upward. Ankles. Legs. Hips. Let the body receive the morning.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Now notice your hands. Maybe warmth. Maybe stillness. Maybe a quiet pulse. Let the hands wake in their own way.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Now the chest. Feel the small movement of breathing. The morning coming in. The morning going out.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Let the face soften a little. Forehead. Eyes. Jaw. Let there be a little more room.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Good. The body can rise slowly. One breath. One inch. One small beginning.
`[pause — 5 breaths]`
Now sense the whole body here. Feet, hands, breath, face. Awake enough. Present enough. Ready enough to begin gently.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Take one last easy breath. And when it feels right, let your eyes open. Softly. The day can begin from here.

# 2. Spark · `spark.m4a`
**Purpose:** a light morning lift · **Target:** ~3 min

**Seed:** Energy doesn’t have to burst in. Sometimes it arrives as one clean breath, then another.

**Script:**
Let’s begin with the smallest spark. Let the morning come close for a moment.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Feel the breath as it is now. Easy in. Easy out.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Now take a fuller breath in. Let it fill the chest a little. And let it out with a soft sigh.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Again. Breath in, a little brighter. Breath out, a little clearer.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
One more like that. Full enough to wake something. Soft enough to stay kind.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Now let the breath settle back into its own rhythm. Feel what’s here. Maybe a little warmth. Maybe a quiet buzz. Maybe just one small bit of morning returning.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Let that be enough. A spark doesn’t need to become a fire all at once. It can simply be a first light.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Now sense the face. The chest. The hands. Let the body know the day has started.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Take one last easy breath. And when it feels right, open your eyes. Carry the small spark with you.

# 3. Clear the Lens · `clear-the-lens.m4a`
**Purpose:** focus primer · **Target:** ~4 min

**Seed:** Before the day fills the room, let’s clear one small window for the mind.

**Script:**
Let’s begin simply. Let the breath be the first clear thing.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Feel where the breath is easiest to notice. Maybe at the nose. Maybe in the chest. Maybe in the belly. Choose the place that feels most natural.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Rest your attention there. Breath arriving. Breath leaving.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Each breath gives the mind one small window. One clear place to look through.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
If attention moves, let it return with the next breath. Lightly. Like placing a cup back on a table.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Breath in. Breath out. A little more space. A little more clear light.
`[pause — 5 breaths]`
Now stay with a few breaths quietly. I’ll leave some room here.
`[pause — 6 breaths]`
Good. Notice the quality of the mind now. Maybe clearer. Maybe steadier. Maybe simply more awake.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Let that clarity stay light. Take one last breath. And when it feels right, open your eyes. Begin from this clearer place.

# 4. Open with Warmth · `open-with-warmth.m4a`
**Purpose:** warmth, openness, self-compassion · **Target:** ~5 min

**Seed:** Warmth can start very small: one kind breath inward, one kind thought outward.

**Script:**
Let’s begin gently. Feel the breath coming in. Feel the breath going out.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Let the next breath arrive with a little warmth. Nothing big. Just a small kindness in the way you breathe.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Now bring to mind one simple wish for yourself today. Something small. Something kind. Maybe: May I move through today gently. Or: May I meet today with warmth.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Let the words be simple. Let them land lightly.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Now think of one person you care about. Picture them in an ordinary moment. Nothing perfect. Just them, being themselves.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Send them one small wish too. May your day be kind to you. May something soften for you today.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Notice how warmth moves. It doesn’t need much. A breath. A thought. A small turning toward care.
`[pause — 5 breaths]`
Now let the image fade. Keep only the warmth.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Feel it in the chest. The hands. The face.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Let this be the tone you carry into the day. Soft, but not small. Kind, but still awake.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Take one last easy breath. And when it feels right, open your eyes. Let the morning meet you warmly.

# 5. Set the Day · `set-the-day.m4a`
**Purpose:** gentle intention-setting · **Target:** ~4 min

**Seed:** Today doesn’t need a grand plan. Just a gentle direction to return to.

**Script:**
Let’s begin with the day still open. Before it gathers its shape, take one easy breath.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Feel the morning as a clean beginning. Not empty. Just open.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Now ask quietly: What kind of direction would feel good today?
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Let the answer come softly. It might be focus. Peace. Energy. Confidence. Rest. Balance. Or something with no perfect name.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Choose the word, or the feeling, that feels closest. Let it sit in the breath.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Breath in. Direction. Breath out. Ease.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Now imagine one ordinary moment later today. A doorway. A message. A cup. A conversation. A small pause between things.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Let your direction meet that moment. Not as a rule. As a quiet reminder.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Good. The day has a thread now. Light enough to carry. Clear enough to return to.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Take one last breath with that direction. And when it feels right, open your eyes. Begin gently.

# 6. Steady the Storm · `steady-the-storm.m4a`
**Purpose:** settling a rough, sleepless, or anxious morning enough to begin · **Target:** ~5 min
*(Voice extra slow and low here.)*

**Seed:** Some mornings arrive unevenly. We can begin softly, one slower breath at a time.

**Script:**
Let’s begin softly. Let the morning be small for now. Just this breath.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Feel the breath coming in. Then let the breath out a little longer.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
Again. Easy breath in. Longer breath out.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Let the out-breath take its time. Like warm water pouring slowly from a cup.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Stay with that for a few breaths. In, easy. Out, slow.
`[pause — 6 breaths]`
Good. Now feel the body making contact with what’s underneath you. The bed. The chair. The floor. Whatever is holding you this morning.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Let the weight of the body settle there. A little more with each out-breath.
`[pause — 5 breaths]`
Now bring attention to the hands. Feel the hands. Warm, cool, still, restless — however they are.
`[pause — 3 breaths]`
Then the face. Forehead. Eyes. Jaw. Let the next out-breath soften the face a little.
`[pause — 4 breaths]`
Good. The morning can begin from this smaller place. A slower breath. A steadier body. A little more ground.
`[pause — 5 breaths]`
Stay here quietly for a few breaths. I’ll leave room.
`[pause — 7 breaths]`
Now take one final slow breath in. And a long, easy breath out.
`[pause — 2 breaths]`
When it feels right, open your eyes. Start with the next small thing.
